# Who subscribes, who buys, and what each run should improve

The primary commercial outcome is incremental paid MasterGrowbot AI subscriptions. The newsletter earns repeat attention by reporting on cannabis and technology. It can serve readers who will never buy the app. Keep the best relevant story even in a week with no natural product offer.

MasterGrowbot AI offers photo reports and a persistent grow journal through its paid app offering. Link the correct store for the device. | Tool result or export → app CTA → attributable paid subscription. Count repeat use and reader questions privately. |

Pro includes photo analysis and journaling. The site describes short video analysis as an iPhone Premium feature. Check current store, version, territory, plan, trial eligibility and entitlement before any offer. A free app download, a Pro trial and a settled paid subscription are different stages. ## Weekly scorecard

Use one private row per issue/post and placement. Keep identical UTMs from the external URL through the in-site path. Store the actual sourceForm, landing path, selected desk, audience intent when known, and calendar window. Use unique `utm_content` per byline and asset. Do not send raw email, notes, image contents or personal identifiers to GA4.

| Stage | Metric and denominator | Source and action |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Impressions at seven days; tagged qualified sessions per placement | Buffer/LinkedIn then GA4. Count comparable landing sessions. High reach with few visits calls for a stronger demonstrated payoff or clearer destination. |
| Use | Tool starts, outputs, copies, exports or shares per tagged session | Check actual GA4 event names in website code. Home journal events use home_journal_start/copy/export/share/app_click. Workbench has select/use/copy/export/share/signup_request. Expose a worked result earlier when users leave before reaching it. |
| Request | Distinct newsletter form requests per qualified session; compare by source and placement | Formspree. A request following an export suggests a relevant offer. If use is healthy but requests lag, test the reason to read the *next edition*. |
| Consent | Matched verified confirmations per distinct request; new eligible people minus losses; total distinct eligible by cadence | Authenticated receipt + intake + Resend and opt-out replies. Exclude QA and dedupe. Treat new, pending, upgraded, unsubscribed and suppressed separately. If requests do not confirm, test delivery and the confirmation step first. |
| Commercial intent | Tagged offer/store visits per qualified reader, replies expressing a specific need | GA4 and private reply classification. Use relevant product links once. Inspect the offer and destination when qualified clicks produce little downstream activity. |
| Revenue | New paid subscriptions, renewals and refunds separately; attributed net proceeds per placement, issue and four-week period | Match verified provider transactions or campaign reports where supported. Use actual fees/refunds and currency. Record unresolved costs as unknown. Deduplicate by transaction/order across channels. RevenueCat, app stores, Whop and KDP are separate sources. |
| Guardrails | Unsubscribes, complaints, bounces, homepage download CTA rate, publishing time and incremental spend | Protect consent and the app sales page. Investigate a complaint or broken delivery immediately. Keep the experiment within existing free tools. |

Use direct campaign evidence where available. A tagged store click followed by a general rise in aggregate app sales cannot identify that buyer. Report attributed and unattributed revenue separately, record reporting lag and leave unavailable values unknown. Never match customer email across products to guess a sale. Distinguish purchase date, refund date and payout period. Avoid comparing gross customer spend on one platform with net proceeds on another. When the denominator is zero, report counts and an undefined rate.

**At every run:** read the previous hypothesis, check its observation date, enter actual counts, and record one decision to keep, change or retire the experiment. Compare same-age posts and comparable issues. Pool four weeks when the counts are small. A public peer's engagement does not reveal its sales. Prefer the action nearest to verified revenue with enough evidence to improve it. If a monetization link is broken, repair or reroute within scope before increasing traffic. If buyers arrive but traffic is scarce, reuse the best verified result in an available LinkedIn slot. Hold the homepage download CTA as a guardrail.

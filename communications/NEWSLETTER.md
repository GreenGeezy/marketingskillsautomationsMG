# Tuesday operating instructions

**Outcome.** Deliver one useful weekly edition of Cannabis AI News & Tools to readers who chose that cadence. Keep the owner able to review or hold it. The Tuesday 10 a.m. run prepares the edition; actual reader delivery follows the review window.

1. Read the current writer, issue ledger, pending holds/edits, prior customer broadcast, outstanding archive and last experiment. Compare the prior issue's confirmations by source, reader replies, product clicks, paid conversions, net revenue and losses. Pull one recent peer edition in rotation from Cultivated, The Rundown or Tim Ferriss's 5-Bullet Friday. Record the one editorial choice worth testing (e.g., where a source link makes an assertion easier to check). Reuse research; avoid copying copy or layouts.
2. Write 200–350 words. Start with the interesting observation, reported event or decision; explain what changes for a reader and what remains uncertain. Include two or three dated, linked primary-source developments and one relevant mainstream tool in **AI Advantage**, subtitle **AI Tips & Tricks**. Try the workflow on permissible sample input, show what it returned and how to check it independently. A strictly business lead can carry a short at-home application. Rotate the lead across readers and subjects. One practical reader reply question. Include at most one clearly relevant owned offer, alongside the applicable practical resource. Avoid invented experiences, forced links to competitors, placeholder headlines and unsupported implications. Recheck facts, dates, destinations, availability and prices just before scheduling.
3. Produce clean mobile HTML and matching plain text. Use descriptive links, readable images-off rendering, alt text and a small real output or labeled illustration. Use the private HTML preview and editorial sample where available; `linkedin-authority/drafts/fieldnotes-weekly-preview.html` and `workbench-weekly-sample.md` are references, not send-ready issues. Keep the held November edition and its excerpts private. Use tagged links to the home tool or appropriate business desk. Render provider unsubscribe placeholder `{{{RESEND_UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}}}` and the owner's approved private-configured business postal footer. Verify provider one-click unsubscribe and working reply routing after material changes.
4. **Build the eligible audience privately.** Reconcile Formspree requests with authenticated Gmail consent receipts and the original promise. Historical documented monthly permissions retain their original cadence. Recheck replies requesting opt-out, Resend unsubscribes/suppressions, bounces and complaints. Exclude owner, tests, duplicate addresses and unresolved records. Normalize case and whitespace for exact identity; avoid collapsing different people across providers on guesswork. An existing suppression dominates consent evidence. Never add an address book, prospect, LinkedIn follower, app account, purchase or raw Sheet row merely because it exists. With a send-only website key, use the authorized Resend dashboard for segments. Verify actual membership and exclusions before releasing.
5. Weekly explicit opt-ins may receive one edition each weekly cycle. Monthly is the first-Tuesday edition from December 1, 2026 onward, maximum one **customer send per recipient per calendar month** in Mexico City, unless the reader explicitly upgrades. Check actual prior delivery, scheduled commitments and planned delivery month when recovering a delayed issue. Dedupe overlap within a single edition; do not count an upgrade as acquisition. Ambiguous cadence remains held. For zero eligible customers send the owner-only preview and finish analysis, keeping the customer draft private.
6. **Prepare one customer broadcast.** Use the edition's original local Tuesday date as stable identity. Resume any existing Resend object; record ID, subject, audience definition, body/version, sender and planned delivery privately. Set public From and Reply-To from SYSTEMS.md. Send the owner the exact rendered preview plus recipient count, delivery time and private edit/hold link (review metadata can accompany the preview separately). Verify actual inbox delivery, capture timestamp and content version, then choose delivery at least **48 elapsed hours afterwards**. Tuesday 10:20 local preview delivery means Thursday 10:20 local at the earliest that week. No owner approval click is required. Persist the selected schedule and verify remote state. Check suppressions before scheduling and rely on provider unsubscribe enforcement through send. If a manually received opt-out cannot be enforced before send, hold the broadcast.
7. **Edits, holds and recovery.** The owner can edit/hold the provider broadcast during review. Honor a hold immediately. Use the provider's scheduling/unschedule control or verified equivalent. A material edit needs a new exact preview and review window; cancel or reschedule the stale version first and verify it cannot go out. Verify the private hold mechanism can actually prevent release while the scheduler is idle. A flag in a local note alone cannot cancel a provider-scheduled broadcast. If the replacement cannot enforce those controls, keep the draft private until they work. For uncertain acknowledgments, inspect Resend ID, schedule, final HTML and audience before retrying. Only repeat a deliverability rehearsal after a material system change, with an isolated consenting test.
8. **Archive once.** Inspect remote customer broadcast ID, final sent version, send state and evidence of eligible customer delivery. “Sent” can describe an owner rehearsal. Exclude previews, held versions, test-only sends and empty audiences. Reconcile the edition against the shared archive ledger. Publish the final customer-facing content, public sources and links under the site's publication schema; remove recipient-specific tokens, unsubscribe URLs, private edit links and tracking identifiers. Preserve the final editorial wording. Build/deploy using the site's authorized workflow, verify the public issue/URL and record it. A delayed Thursday delivery remains pending for the next verified check. Either job can reconcile it; only one writes the archive.

**Example recovery:** A successor sees a scheduled edition and no local success receipt. Read the existing broadcast by ID, match the preview version, recipient segment and time, and record the verified schedule. Do not create another broadcast. If it was delivered to the owner alone, record the rehearsal privately and leave the archive unchanged.

# 10x qualified-traffic acquisition sprint — October 17, 2026

The objective is 10x website traffic from likely MasterGrowbot AI buyers, with increased paid subscriptions as the business outcome. No growth multiplier or deadline is guaranteed. The initial sprint lasts 14 days from activation; evaluate traffic over settled 28-day windows and supported subscription/revenue cohorts over 60–90 days. Search recrawling and editorial review impose delays that faster inference cannot remove.

## The 80/20 choice

Concentrate 80% of working time on buyer-intent software pages, their conversion paths, evidence/measurement and verified indexing problems. Spend 20% on relevant distribution and discovering the next distinct comparison. The existing homepage and AI-app guide lead app-related acquisition; preserve their recent changes while building distinct comparison resources around them. Read actual private baselines rather than stale fields in current.json.

Three jobs run daily at 09:45, 10:45 and 11:45 America/Mexico_City. Each has a maximum 50 minutes, a shared lock, one attempt per job/day and an absolute 12:45 cutoff. There is no value in spending all available time if the next action lacks evidence. Research and preparation may run daily; full audits and performance comparisons do not.

## Goal definition and decision rules

Freeze the pre-migration settled 28-day GSC baseline in private state. Track 10x GSC web clicks as a search-traffic milestone, but separately establish total GA4 website sessions and the buyer-intent landing-page cohort. GSC clicks are not unique visitors. Primary quality guardrails: qualified session mix, store-click-session rate, then attributed trials/paid conversion where available. Preserve unknown attribution as null.

The revenue relationship is qualified visits × store-click-session rate × store-to-install rate × install-to-paid rate × net subscriber value. Missing factors prevent a revenue forecast. At day14 inspect delivery/indexing and early signals; at day28 after recrawl evaluate matched traffic; at 60–90 days assess attribution. If three new comparisons receive no meaningful impressions after adequate indexing/maturation, revisit demand/intent/distribution rather than increase the page quota.

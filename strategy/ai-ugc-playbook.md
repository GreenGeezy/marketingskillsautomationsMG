# AI UGC playbook: "Sage", the AI grow host

**Goal:** about 1 video a day for 30 days that drives MasterGrowbot AI installs. Production runs on Google AI Pro (Gemini 4 Neon in Antigravity, Flow/Veo video). Posting and replies run through Muse.

## Rules (they keep the account alive)
1. Sage is openly an AI host. Turn on Instagram's "AI info" label on every post. Bio: "AI grow host 🌱 Real plant scans from MasterGrowbot AI".
2. Sage never claims a personal grow or result. Sage narrates; the screen recordings are the proof.
3. Real app footage only for app claims: record actual scans on your phone.
4. Muse replies to comments and inbound DMs. Use comment-to-DM ("Comment SCAN"). No cold DMs.
5. App mention in about 1 of every 3 videos; the rest are pure grow tips, so the account isn't flagged as spam.


## Step 2: assemble each video (about 10 minutes each, CapCut or Instagram's editor)
1. 0 to 3 s: Sage's hook clip.
2. 3 to 20 s: your phone screen recording of the matching real scan or tip, with big captions.
3. 20 to 25 s: Sage's closing line (generate a second clip, or use text over the recording).
4. Turn on the AI label, then hand it to Muse to schedule and handle comments.

## What to measure (weekly)
- Views per video and follows.
- "SCAN" comments.
- App Store installs from the bio link: use the UTM `utm_campaign=sage_ugc` on the store link.
- Subscriptions in RevenueCat against the Nov 22 baseline.

Kill or change the hook style after 7 videos with fewer than 1,000 views each. Double down on whatever hook pattern breaks 10k views.

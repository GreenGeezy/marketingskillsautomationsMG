# Authorized outreach pilot: October 9, 2026

The human explicitly authorized sends without additional review using Gmail from mastergrowbotai@gmail.com. Verify connected sender before sending; never substitute another account. Restrict scope to relevant editorial/resource links and software listings for MasterGrowbot. No paid services, purchased lists, tracking pixels, mass mail, invented claims, paid dofollow links or link exchanges.

Start with at most five new prospects per Monday–Sunday week in America/Mexico_City, one contact per organization. Choose a relevant live article/resource, concrete reader benefit, publicly verified editorial/business email and suitable verified live destination. Existing backlink exports may be reused; public research is not a complete competitor backlink export. Research up to ten competitors once and cache dated evidence privately. Reject weak prospects rather than fill a quota.

Before each send check the private outreach.json ledger and narrow Gmail searches for existing contact, replies, bounces, opt-outs or ambiguous attempts. Write a pending attempt with a stable prospect/campaign identity before sending; record returned message/thread ID immediately afterward. If result is ambiguous, mark unknown and reconcile Sent before retrying. An API send receipt proves sending, not delivery. Label unbounced mail presumed delivered only after a bounce observation period and report that limitation.

Messages are concise and individually tailored, with a specific reference and truthful reader benefit. Identify MasterGrowbot affiliation and make declining easy. At most one follow-up after 7–10 days; first check the tracked thread, bounces and suppression list. Never follow up on a decline, opt-out, bounce or substantive reply. Stop after the follow-up; suppression persists across campaigns.

Record prospect URL/contact source/relevance, recipient organization, message and thread IDs, send/follow-up dates, reply classification, bounce/opt-out, verified live placement, referral sessions/store clicks, supported paid attribution, and actual elapsed effort/available usage. Do not expose private records publicly. Use canonical editorial links; campaign-labelled outreach links may identify visits where appropriate, never add internal UTMs.

Evaluate after 20 delivered (or explicitly labelled presumed-delivered) initial messages and at least 30 days. Scale to ten new prospects per week only with at least two positive replies and one verified relevant placement, no unresolved delivery issues. These are operating gates, not statistical proof or promised rates. Otherwise revise targeting/offer before another five-prospect batch. Pause new sends on a spam complaint or two hard bounces in rolling 20 attempts; diagnose before resumption. Do not change the automation status merely to pause outreach.

Track positive-reply and placement rates with explicit denominators, referral-to-store-click rates when measured, and paid subscriptions only with supported attribution. Compare effort per positive reply/placement; tokens or cost unavailable must remain null. No open tracking. Refresh prospect research monthly or when the qualified queue is exhausted, not on every run. Read only tracked threads for reply monitoring.
